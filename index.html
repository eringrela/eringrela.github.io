Erin Grela

Hello, I'm Erin! I'm a fifth-year PhD student in the MIT Economics Department. I work at the intersection of development and environmental economics. My projects focus on how we can more accurately quantify damages from climate change in developing settings, given the adaptations that are feasible in these settings. They draw on our understanding of informal economies and take seriously the structure and timing of adverse weather.
